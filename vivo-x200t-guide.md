## 📋 Complete Specifications

### Display & Design
- **Screen:** 6.67-inch AMOLED, 1.5K resolution (1260 × 2800)
- **Refresh Rate:** 120Hz HDR10+ support
- **Brightness:** 1600nits peak, 5000nits HDR
- **Protection:** Schott Xensation glass
- **Build:** Aluminum frame, glass back, IP68/IP69 water resistance
- **Dimensions:** 160.01 × 74.29 × 7.99 mm
- **Weight:** 203 grams
- **Colors:** Seaside Lilac, Stellar Black

### Performance
- **Processor:** MediaTek Dimensity 9400+ (3nm)
- **CPU:** Octa-core (1×3.73GHz + 3×3.3GHz + 4×2.4GHz)
- **GPU:** Immortalis-G925 MC12
- **RAM:** 12GB LPDDR5X Ultra
- **Storage:** 256GB/512GB UFS 4.0
- **AnTuTu Score:** 28,78,376 (benchmark)

### Camera System
- **Triple Rear Camera:** 50MP + 50MP + 50MP (All Zeiss)
  - **Main:** 50MP Sony IMX921, f/1.57, 23mm
  - **Telephoto:** 50MP Sony IMX882, f/2.57, 70mm (3x optical zoom)
  - **Ultra-wide:** 50MP Samsung ISOCELL JN1, f/2.0, 15mm
- **Front Camera:** 32MP KD1 sensor
- **Video:** 4K @60fps all cameras
- **Features:** ZEISS HyperZoom 100x, All-Pixels Focus Technology

### Battery & Charging
- **Capacity:** 6200mAh (Typical: 6200 mAh, Rated: 6060 mAh)
- **Charging:** 90W wired + 40W wireless
- **Special Features:** Bypass charging for gaming, 3rd Gen Silicon Anode technology

### Software & OS
- **OS:** Android 16-based OriginOS 6
- **Updates:** 5 years OS updates, 7 years security updates
- **Special Features:** Origin Island (cross-device productivity), AI SuperLink, One-Tap Transfer to iPhone
